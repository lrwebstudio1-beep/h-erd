Privacy policy

Last updated 8 November 2026

This page explains what personal data is processed when you visit the Hotel Erdano website or contact us, why it is processed and what your rights are, under Albanian Law no. 124/2024 “On Personal Data Protection” and, for visitors from the European Union, Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR).

Who we are

The controller of your data is Hotel & Restaurant Erdano, Rruga Butrinti 39, Sarandë 9701, Albania.

  • Email: hotelerdano2025@gmail.com
  • Phone: +355 68 495 0566

What this website does not do

The website has no user accounts, visitor statistics, advertising or tracking cookies, and the website itself stores nothing on your device. Fonts, photos and videos are loaded from the website itself.

The booking form does not send us anything. It only prepares a message with your dates and number of guests, which opens in WhatsApp or your email app. The message is sent only if you send it.

The data you give us

When you write to us or call us, we receive the data you give us:

  • your name;
  • your phone number or email address;
  • your dates of stay, the number of guests and the content of your message.

We use this data only to reply to you and to manage your booking. The legal basis is preparing and carrying out your booking. When you stay at the hotel, we may ask for further data where the law requires it. We do not sell your data.

Hosting on Cloudflare

The website is served by Cloudflare. To display the website and protect it from attacks, Cloudflare processes technical data from each visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a secure, working website.

Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies (for example __cf_bm and cf_clearance) to tell visitors apart from automated programs. Cloudflare’s privacy policy

Services from other companies

These services are used only if you choose to open them, and their own policies apply:

  • Google Maps: the map loads only after you press “Show map”. Google then receives your IP address and may set cookies. Links to Google Maps also open Google’s service. Google’s policy
  • WhatsApp: if you write to us on WhatsApp, your message passes through that service. WhatsApp’s policy
  • Gmail: our email is provided by Google, so messages you send us are stored with Google.
  • Instagram and Facebook: the links open our profiles on these networks. Meta’s policy

Transfers abroad

Cloudflare, Google and Meta may process data in other countries, including the United States.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data only as long as needed for your booking and for our legal obligations, such as accounting.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for access to your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing and to have it transferred, and to object to its processing. Write to us at hotelerdano2025@gmail.com.

You can complain to the Commissioner for the Right to Information and Protection of Personal Data in Albania. Visitors from the European Union can also contact the data protection authority in their own country.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will be updated.
