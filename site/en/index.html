Frequently asked questions

How do I book a room?

Directly with us, with no commission: message us on WhatsApp or call +355 68 495 0566, or email hotelerdano2025@gmail.com. You talk to us, not a call centre.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 14:00 to 23:00 and check-out is by 10:00. The front desk is open from 07:00 to 23:30.

How far is the hotel from the promenade and Ksamil?

The Saranda promenade is an 8-minute walk. By car, Ksamil is about 20 minutes away, Butrint about 25 and the Blue Eye about 30.

What do the rooms have?

Every room has air conditioning, a TV, a mini fridge, free Wi-Fi (100+ Mbps) and a private bathroom. Most rooms have a sea-view balcony.

Can I eat at the restaurant if I’m not staying at the hotel?

Yes. The terrace restaurant serves breakfast, lunch and dinner to everyone. For a table at sunset, especially in July and August, message us ahead.

Are pets or smoking allowed?

Pets are not allowed. Rooms and indoor areas are non-smoking.
